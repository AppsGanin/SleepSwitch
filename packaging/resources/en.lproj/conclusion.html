SleepSwitch is installed and running — look for the icon on the right side of the menu bar.

Left click the icon to turn the mode on and off.
Right click opens the menu with settings.

The icon shows the state: a moon means normal mode, a coffee cup means sleep is blocked, a triangle means the mode is only partially on.

To have SleepSwitch start after a reboot, tick «Open at login» in its menu.

If your Mac asks for a password when you reopen the lid, that is the separate screen lock setting — this installer does not touch it. Turn it off in System Settings → Lock Screen → «Require password after screen saver begins or display is turned off» → «Never».

To uninstall: drag SleepSwitch.app to the Trash and run sudo rm /etc/sudoers.d/sleepswitch.
