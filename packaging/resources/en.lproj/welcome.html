SleepSwitch is a menu bar toggle that stops your MacBook from sleeping — both when you close the lid and when the idle timer runs out.

This installer will do two things:

  • Install SleepSwitch.app into your Applications folder and launch it — the icon appears on the right side of the menu bar.
  • Add a sudo rule to /etc/sudoers.d/sleepswitch so the mode toggles without a password prompt. The rule allows your account exactly two commands — pmset -a disablesleep 0 and pmset -a disablesleep 1 — and nothing else.

You can skip the second one: click «Customize» on the installation type step. macOS will then ask for an administrator password every time you toggle the mode.

Requires macOS 13 or newer.
